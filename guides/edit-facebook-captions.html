How to Edit Facebook Captions After Uploading

Fix caption errors on your Facebook video, and upload a corrected SRT file.

Caption errors are embarrassing, but they are also easy to fix if you know where the editor is. Facebook lets creators edit automatic captions and upload a corrected SRT file, so a typo does not have to stay on your video forever. This guide shows how to find the editor and make clean corrections.

Find the caption editor

Open your video in Meta Business Suite or in Facebook's video settings, and look for Captions or Subtitles. If the video has automatic captions, you should see an option to review and edit them. The editor shows the caption text alongside the video, so you can check each line as it plays.

Fix errors line by line

Play the video and correct each caption as you hear it. Fix names, numbers, and words that change the meaning first, then fix spelling and punctuation. Split long lines into two shorter ones, and merge fragments that belong to the same sentence. Save your changes before you leave the page.

Upload a corrected SRT file

If you have many corrections, it is faster to download the SRT file, edit it in a text editor, and upload it again. Make sure the file keeps its SRT structure, with numbers, timestamps, and blank lines. Upload the corrected file in the captions section and check that Facebook accepts it.

Check the result

After saving, watch the video with captions on and confirm the fixes appear. Ask a colleague to watch it too, because a fresh pair of eyes catches errors you have read past. If the old captions still show, clear your browser cache or wait a few minutes for the change to spread.

Putting it into practice

Start with a single, short example and work through the steps above from beginning to end. Do not try to do everything at once, and do not use an important or irreplaceable video for your first attempt. Once the first try works, repeat the process with a second example, and you will find the steps become familiar quickly. Keep your files organised as you go, give them clear names, and check the finished result on the device where you plan to use it, whether that is your phone, your computer, or a tablet.

Common mistakes to avoid

The most common mistake is rushing: pasting the wrong link, picking the lowest quality, or downloading from the first site that appears in a search without checking whether it is trustworthy. The second is ignoring storage and file names, which leaves you with a folder full of files called download-1 and download-2 that you cannot tell apart. The third is forgetting the creator: always credit the person who made the original, keep personal copies personal, and ask before you reuse someone else's work in public.

The easiest way is to follow the steps in this guide in order, starting with the first section. Copy the correct link, use a reputable tool, and check the preview before you download or save anything. That avoids most problems and gives you a clean result on the first try.

Do I need to install anything to how to edit facebook captions after uploading?

No. Everything in this guide works in a normal web browser on your phone or computer, without installing an app. If a site asks you to install software or enter a password, close it and use a different tool.

What should I do if it does not work?

Go back to the first step and check the link, your connection, and your storage. Try a different browser, clear your cache, and try again. If the video or post is private or has been deleted, no tool can fetch it, and you should ask the creator for a copy instead.
